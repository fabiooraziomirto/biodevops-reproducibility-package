Clinical-facts annotation packet

Annotate independently. Do not inspect benchmark labels, expected failure probes, model outputs, existing ontology mappings, or another rater's file. For each narrative, record only explicitly supported facts. Choose a numeric SNOMED CT code, event/exposure booleans, harm_outcome (none|harm|death), assertion_status (confirmed|suspected|negated|mention-only|absent), and a minimal FHIR-shaped fragment (Observation or DetectedIssue; id; status). Return only the completed CSV; disagreements are adjudicated separately.
